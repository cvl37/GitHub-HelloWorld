About Me

An Inspector's Career. A Consultant's Focus.

I'm Colin van Lier — aerodrome inspector, auditor and certification expert based in Blaricum, The Netherlands. My career spans more than two decades of frontline inspection work, covering civil aerodromes, offshore helidecks, and helicopter-only operations across the Netherlands and beyond.

I work across the full range of applicable frameworks: ICAO Annex 14, EASA Part-ADR, the Dutch RVGLT, Wet Luchtvaart, and Besluit Luchtverkeer 2014 — as well as aviation policy development and regulatory advice for authorities and operators alike.

As an independent consultant I bring authority-level expertise directly to airports, ground operators, offshore companies and governmental bodies — without the overhead and generalism of a large consultancy firm.
